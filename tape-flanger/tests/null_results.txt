Null of 1.2.5 against 1.2.4, both rendered through the real AU. s48b128: 48 kHz stereo, 128 frame blocks. s44b64: 44.1 kHz stereo, 64. s96b512: 96 kHz stereo, 512. m48b256: 48 kHz mono, 256. auto: random automation and preset changes. Input is speech, a sweep and silence. Earlier nulls are in git history.

1.2.5 changes what happens after a control moves: the glide now lands on the value set, where 1.2.4 stopped short of it. NullRender picks each preset after the plug-in is initialised, as a host does, so every preset glides there from the defaults. The default and Manual Ride (which moves nothing that glides) are bit identical to 1.2.4 at all four formats: 8 files. The other 48 preset files and the 7 auto files differ by what 1.2.4 was missing: the largest, Off Zero Double at 96 kHz, peaks at -45.0 dBFS (rms -68.4 dBFS), where 1.2.4 held Delay 0.0003 ms short of 14 ms. In PluginTest, each preset picked while playing now matches the same preset set before prepare at -156.9 dB or better (1.2.4: -38.0 to -59.9 dB on 12 of 13).

Hashes of the 1.2.5 renders are in tests/baseline/renders-1.2.5.sha256.
