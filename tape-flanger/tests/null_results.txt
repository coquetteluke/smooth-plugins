Null of 1.2.7 against 1.2.6, both rendered through the real AU. s48b128: 48 kHz stereo, 128 frame blocks. s44b64: 44.1 kHz stereo, 64. s96b512: 96 kHz stereo, 512. m48b256: 48 kHz mono, 256. auto: random automation and preset changes. Input is speech, a sweep and silence. Earlier nulls are in git history.

The default and every preset at all four formats are bit identical to 1.2.6: 56 files. The 7 auto files differ, as they must: the random automation jumps LFO Phase across 0/360 degrees and picks presets (which set it to 0) from high values, and 1.2.7 glides the short way round where 1.2.6 swept back through the whole cycle. A build of 1.2.7 with only that change taken out was bit identical to 1.2.6 on all 63 files, so nothing else changed what NullRender plays.

Hashes of the 1.2.7 renders are in tests/baseline/renders-1.2.7.sha256.
