## 0.7.2, 2026-11-13

Full working set on the installed 0.7.2 against 0.7.0 (same check file): 205 failing lines in both, none newly failing, none newly passing.

- The voice source's slope moves with the formants again from 4 times the pitch up, as in 0.7.0 (throat-r1-1 is open again). Each fix built alone on takes 04, 05 and 12 showed these came from this one. Taking the fall off only on clearly voiced frames, or only 60% of it, still broke them. What goes back with it: on a plain -6 dB per octave source with no formants, Level Match off, 2 to 6 kHz against the input, Length 0.70 -0.21 to +2.86 dB and 1.50 +0.82 to -2.68 dB (the hunt's limit 1; that line is INFO now, not a check), and 6 real vocal lines that 0.7.0 failed and 0.7.1 passed fail again, all at Small Bright and Tiny (Small Bright contrast above 200 Hz median 2.50 to 3.14 dB and p90 5.62 to 6.29 dB, limit 3 and 6; Tiny's contrast in the vocoded, 130 to 200 Hz and rap readings back to 0.7.0's 3.70 to 7.13 dB).
- A consonant is still shaped the same whatever note came before it (kept from 0.7.1), but the change from the model to the envelope on unvoiced frames now sits as for a 250 Hz note instead of 60 Hz. At 60 Hz Big Chest's consonant smear after quiet went over its limit, p90 2.96 to 3.01 dB (limit 3); at 250 Hz it reads 2.98 (150 Hz 2.99, 400 Hz 2.98). That costs a seventh line 0.7.1 had fixed: Tiny's clean phrase starts, p95 9 to 11 ms (limit 10), as in 0.7.0. Hiss band passed 1 to 3 kHz, after a 900 Hz note against after a 100 Hz note: 0.02 dB (limit 0.5, 0.7.0 1.24).
- Kept from 0.7.1 unchanged: the edge handle, the saved preset, ratio text, state values with no number, controls that change nothing, Reset.
- Throat's own tests: 269 pass, 0 fail (271 in 0.7.1; the two source slope lines are INFO now). NullTest's renders were saved again from 0.7.2 (the saved 0.2.0 session file kept), 59 pass. Against what 0.7.0 played, 0.7.2 differs at Big Chest -31.9, Pinched -30.3, Giant -44.3 and Subtle Blend -35.6 dBFS and plays the default exactly; against 0.7.1's renders, Big Chest -28.8, Pinched -27.6, Giant -28.3, Subtle Blend -32.4 and the session -28.6 dBFS, the default and Full Whisper exact.

Throat's own tests pass (271, 0 fail; 246 before plus 25 new checks). Hiss band passed 1 to 3 kHz, after a 900 Hz note against after a 100 Hz note, Level Match off: Length 0.75 1.24 to 0.02 dB, Length 1.20 0.81 to 0.02 dB (limit 0.5).
- Moving a control that does nothing in the setting it is in changes nothing. Giant, Air tone moved under audio: -25.9 dB from the still render to -335 dB; Length 1.10, Position 3 moved: -38.7 to -335 dB (limit -100).
- The chosen preset is saved with the session and named again on reload, without being applied again. ## 0.7.0, 2026-11-12

Luke left this call to the lead on 2026-11-11. A change to how every setting but Neutral sounds, in two places: how far a small formant move goes, and how the level is held at the start of a phrase. Throat's own tests pass (246, 0 fail). NullTest's renders were saved again from 0.7.0 (the saved 0.2.0 session file kept); against the 0.6.0 renders, 0.7.0 differs at Big Chest -15.6, Giant -16.3, Subtle Blend -22.9, the session -24.4, Pinched -24.8 and Whisper -25.2 dBFS, and nulls exactly at the default. On real takes 06, 09 and 16, every setting: failing rules 230 to 186, one newly failing (Pinched Throat, consonant smear after quiet, p90 2.77 to 3.24 dB, limit 3).

- Width points and small moves go further. From 4 times the pitch up a voiced frame's move now reads a plain cepstral envelope (every quefrency under 0.6 of the period kept as it is) instead of the envelope through the harmonic tips, which is smoother; a smoother envelope moves less of a formant's shape, and a move of a few percent showed it most. Takes 06, 09, 16, formant move against the tube (limit 0.8 to 1.2): fit of the tube's map Lips 0.60 0.66 to 0.78, Roof 0.60 0.66 to 0.80, Width 0.50 0.66 to 0.84, Pinched Throat 0.60 to 0.76, Rounded Lips 0.74 to 0.86; slope Width 2.00 0.81 to 0.88, Throat 1.60 0.69 to 0.78, Raspy Edge 0.71 to 0.83. Formant scatter for voices over 200 Hz (limit 6.3, 5.7 and 5.5%): Length 0.70 11.5 to 5.7%, Length 0.85 7.8 to 5.4%, Length 1.10 5.9 to 4.8%. Unvoiced frames keep the old envelope, so Throat Precision still steadies the gain on noise.
- Phrase starts. Level Match's quick step read the formant model, which could be far off: on a real vocal at Lips 0.60 the moved first formant landed on the strong second harmonic, the frame went up 13 dB, and the model said nothing changed. It now reads the input's envelope with the gain the frame really gets. The slow step measures only what is left after that. A phrase start is found against the level as it was 43 ms before (a start that rose over 30 ms was missed), and for 320 ms after a rise of 6 dB the quick step follows each frame's real spectrum. Takes 01, 06, 09 at Lips 0.60, Throat 1.60, Length 1.20 and 1.30, Rounded Lips, Big Chest and Giant: late phrase starts p95 21 to 40 ms to 10 ms or less (limit 10), the quietest 5% of phrase starts -3.0 to -4.1 dB to inside -3 dB.
- Dropouts. After a gap in the input, the output stayed within 3 dB of a render without the gap: 3.2 dB over before (take 01, Length 1.20), 0.15 dB now. The phrase start reset of 0.5.0 is kept.
- While a control moves, and for 320 ms after, the quick level step also follows each frame's real spectrum, so the level holds through a preset change (take 01, moving to Giant: 1.1 dB at most over the louder of the dry and the still render, limit 3).
- Not changed, measured: the hot take with Peak Guard off still goes over full scale at the brightening presets (as in 0.6.0; with Peak Guard on it does not), and the 4 steps flagged in the run of control moves on take 06 are in the still render too.

## 0.4.0, 2026-11-11

From the real vocal re-test on the Rode takes. A still setting sounds exactly as in 0.3.1, sample for sample: the default, every preset, and saved sessions.

- Fixed: a host that switches its own bypass on under audio got 32 ms of silence and a click, and the engine started again from nothing. The AU wrapper resets the plug-in in the same call that sets Bypass, and that reset emptied the delay line. A reset that comes right behind Bypass going on is now left out, so the host's bypass fades like the Bypass control does. Every other reset clears as before.
- Fixed: choosing a preset or moving a control while audio plays let the level jump for about a tenth of a second (up to 8 dB on a real vocal), because the gain moved in 40 ms and the slow part of the level match still held the old setting's two second average. While a control is moving the level match now follows frame by frame.
- New: Peak Guard, in Advanced, off by default. Moving formants changes the shape of the wave, so peaks can come out higher than they went in at the same level (up to 3 dB over full scale on a take that peaks just under it). On, the output never peaks higher than the input has. No preset changes it, and off it adds nothing.

## 0.3.0

Its own window. A side view of the vocal tract as one bent tube from the cords to the lips, with the singer's throat in bone and the model in oxide red, both read live from the voice's formants. Five draggable points, a gray realistic range for width and length, Reset, a level meter, F1 to F5 and pitch before and after, the two formant curves, and a quiet latency readout. Sound is unchanged: output nulls bit for bit against 0.2.0 renders on speech, with the window closed, drawing, and being reopened during audio. Sessions from 0.2.0 load as before. The only additions to a saved session are the window's size and whether Advanced is open.

## 0.2.0

First finished version, matched from the start to the control set of the plug-in it replaces. Throat Length, Throat Width, five points along the tract each with its own width, four of them with a position, Breathiness and Breath High Pass, Source and Model Voice Type, Glottal Pulse Width, Vocal Range, Throat Precision, Mix, Output Gain, Level Match, Bypass, and a read-only Output Level. Fourteen presets. Latency 32 ms at 48 and 96 kHz, 34.8 ms at 44.1 and 88.2 kHz, dry path delay-matched. The formant engine lives in src/formant so other plug-ins can copy it.
