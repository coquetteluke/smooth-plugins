Smooth Plugins

15 free Audio Unit plug-ins for Logic Pro, made mostly for vocals.

On the next screens you pick which plug-ins to install (all are ticked to start) and whether they are for every user on this Mac or only for you.

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 13 or newer.

Free and open source under the GNU AGPL v3. Source code and manuals: github.com/coquetteluke/smooth-plugins
